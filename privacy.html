Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy describes how the EXE Agency content publishing tool ("the tool", operated by EXE Agency, exe-agency.com) handles data. The tool is a private application used only by our own team.

Data we access

  • TikTok: when a team member connects a TikTok account that we own or manage, the tool receives an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID and basic profile information. The tool uses these only to publish our own videos to that account and to read statistics (views, likes, comments) of our own videos.
  • Google (Sheets and Drive): the tool reads and updates a single Google Sheet that holds our publishing queue (video links, captions, statuses) and downloads our own video files from Google Drive.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to operate the tool for our own accounts. Tokens are stored on our own private infrastructure and are never shown to third parties. We do not sell data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services) and in the Google Account permissions page. To request deletion of stored data, email gu1gu2gu4@gmail.com.
