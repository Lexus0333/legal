Publish our own videos to our own TikTok accounts in three steps. Internal tool, not offered to the public.

Test mode is on: videos are processed, but nothing is sent to TikTok.
1

Connect to the publishing server

Enter the server address and your access key (provided by the administrator).

The key stays in this browser tab only and is never stored on this site.

2

Connect a TikTok account

You will be sent to TikTok to sign in and approve access (basic profile info and posting of videos), then brought back here.

Complete step 1 to see your accounts.

3

Publish videos

Put video files into the inbox folder (one subfolder per account, for example inbox/account1) and press Publish. Every video is posted in turn, with a short random pause between posts.

StatusVideoAccountResult
Complete step 1 to see the queue.
How does publishing work?

For each video the tool takes a few frames and writes a short on-screen hook and a caption with hashtags (with an AI assistant when it is configured; otherwise from an optional text file with the same name as the video, or from the file name). The hook is added to the first seconds of the video and the file is uploaded through the TikTok Content Posting API (Direct Post) to the connected account. Published videos move to the done folder, failed ones to failed. Until the app is approved by TikTok, posts are private and visible only to the account owner. AI-generated videos are labelled as AI-generated.
