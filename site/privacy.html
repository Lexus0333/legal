Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy describes how the EXE Agency content publishing tool ("the tool", operated by EXE Agency, exe-agency.com) handles data. The tool publishes video content to TikTok accounts that its operator owns or that were connected by their owners with their explicit consent.

Data we access

  • TikTok account connection: when you authorise the tool through TikTok Login, TikTok gives the tool an access token, a refresh token, your open ID and your basic profile information (display name). The requested permissions are user.info.basic (to identify the connected account) and video.publish (to publish videos to that account). The tool does not read your videos, messages, followers or other private data.
  • Publishing records: for each post the tool keeps the file name, the caption, the target account, the time and the result (published or error).
  • Invitation links: a link sent to an account owner contains a single-use random token. We store the token, the account label given to it and its expiry date.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to operate the tool: to publish approved videos to the connected account, to show the operator the status of posts, and to keep the connection working. Tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on the operator's own computer and are never shown on any page or sent to third parties other than TikTok. We do not sell data, share it with advertisers or use it for advertising or profiling. Access to the management console requires a private access key.

Retention, revocation and deletion

You can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services), or ask us to disconnect your account. When an account is disconnected, the tool revokes the token with TikTok and deletes the stored tokens and the link between the account and your TikTok profile. Unused invitation links expire automatically. To request deletion of any other stored data, email gu1gu2gu4@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.
