Each video gets one random hook, one random caption and a few random hashtags from these lists. An account can have its own lists; without them the default lists are used.

Add accounts

Create the accounts in TikTok yourself, then add their names here: this creates their video folders and their own text lists. You can paste up to 1000 names at once, separated by new lines, spaces or commas (letters, digits, “_” and “-” only). After adding, connect each account to TikTok from its row in the Accounts tab.

Invite account owners

The person who owns a TikTok account connects it by themselves through a personal one-time link. They never see your access key. Accounts that are not in the list yet are added automatically.

Connect an account to TikTok

You will be sent to TikTok to sign in and approve access (basic profile info and posting of videos), then brought back here.

New invitations

Send every owner their personal link. A link works once and replaces the previous link of the same account.
