EXE Agency - Content Publishing Tool

EXE Agency (exe-agency.com) operates a tool that organises its video content and publishes it on a schedule to TikTok accounts that it owns or that were connected by their owners with their consent.

An account is connected only by its owner through the official TikTok login, and the owner can revoke access at any time.
